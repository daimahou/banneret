# Curses

- Reserve one caster pip.
- End when dropped, when the enemy dies, or when caster energy reaches zero.
- Dark mechanic associated with purple.

> [!frontier] Agony and its engine
> Example: Agony deals damage each beat. Possible nodes escalate damage and pip cost, or let recasts stack. Life Tap converts health to energy; a priest heals the caster. This engine is intended, but exact skills and values remain examples. A light-and-dark caster could use blood magic Agony and self-healing.
