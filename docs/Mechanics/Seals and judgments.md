# Seals and judgments

- Casting a seal starts a maintained enchantment.
- Breaking it unleashes an immediate judgment and puts the seal on cooldown.
- Paladin mechanic associated with yellow.

> [!frontier] Authored effects
> Individual seals and judgments remain open. Blue charging and purple life payment are cross-colour examples. See Smite for proposed interactions.
