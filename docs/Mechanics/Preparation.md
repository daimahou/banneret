# Preparation

- Active skill repeatedly builds stacks, reserving a pip per use.
- A payoff spends the stacks and frees their pips.
- Overloading allows a larger payoff against energy drain.
- Rogue mechanic associated with green.

> [!frontier] Authored skills
> Example: very fast Envenom builds coatings; coated attacks apply Poison; a payoff such as Rupture spends coatings. Values and exact effects remain illustrative.
