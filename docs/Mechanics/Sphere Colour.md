# Colours

| Working colour | Mechanics |
|---|---|
| Red — strength | Stagger, armour break, banners, block, Bleed |
| Green — dexterity | Combo, preparation, haste, evasion, Poison |
| Blue — intelligence | Channeling, energy shield, Fire |
| Yellow — light | Seals and judgments, Renew, purify |
| Purple — dark | Blood magic, curses, contagion |
| Greyish blue — balance | Forms |

> [!info] Cross-colour node examples
> Purple makes a seal cost life; blue makes it charge each beat; red extends an enchantment to the party at a pip per hero; balance transfers one to an ally for one pip; yellow makes a fire channel remove poison. Green preparation triggered later is a candidate.
