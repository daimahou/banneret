# Forms

- Two forms; swapping is free and changes the active effect.
- Balance mechanic expressed differently by each class.

> [!frontier] Authored forms
> Examples: druid caster/beast; marshal defensive/offensive formations; duelist or monk attacking/guarding stances. Effects and costs remain open.
