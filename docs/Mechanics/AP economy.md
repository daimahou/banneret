# AP economy

A character spends **AP** to schedule a skill. AP regenerates one pip per beat and carries over up to a cap, so a hero can bank more than one and hold it for a burst. The [[Warrior]] holds 2.

> [!fog] Scheduled together, actions each resolve at their own delay.
> A hero with more AP lines up several skills on one beat, e.g. to one-shot an enemy before its heal-over-time ticks.

> [!fog] Most enemies keep AP 1.

> [!fog] AP as class identity
> a rogue with more AP than average bursts all his cooldowns at once and plays around acting a lot.

> [!fog] AP shared out by party size, like Divinity 2's Lone Wolf.
> A lone hero gets more AP than one with followers, so recruiting becomes a trade: more bodies on the rope against fewer actions for the hero.

> [!fog] Skills that change AP: +1 AP for a beat as a burst cooldown, an enemy skill that takes AP away.

> [!fog] Several of one character's medallions on the same tick crowd the rope.
> That stack is also the burst worth showing, so it needs a deliberate look.


> [!fog] A burst weighs AP, energy and cooldown together; some skills cost different amounts of each.

> [!fog] Colored AP, tied to the spheres.
> Open: how the player controls which color he gets, and whether a multi-colored class falls behind a mono-colored one.
