Poison is the slow kill: a scratch now, a corpse later. It never fades.

## Interactions

| With | Idea |
|---|---|
| [[Burn]] | Volatile: fire bursts the stacks at once |
| [[Burn]] | Purifying flame: fire on an ally burns away Poison |
| [[Freeze]] | Frozen pauses Poison; it all lands on thaw |
| [[Bleed]] | Infection: a bleeding, poisoned target can't heal Poison away |
| [[Rot]] | Poison attacks can carry Rot as well as Poison, through nodes |
| [[Preparation]] | At 0 energy, the stored coatings are lost |
| Enemy healers | Blight turns every heal into more Poison |
| [[Channeling]] | A channelled cloud grows with its charges |

## Builds

| Build | Hook | Archetype | Countered by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coating | Envenom stores doses; a flurry dumps them | Assassin | Energy drain |
| Blight | Enemy healing feeds the Poison | Assassin | Cleanse; no enemy healers |
| Enfeeble | Heavy Poison weakens the enemy | Skirmisher | Short fights |
| Plague | Stacks jump on death | Warlock, hexblade | A lone enemy |
| Volatile | Poison then fire for a burst | Assassin + pyromancer | Fire-immune enemies |
| Toxic cloud | Poison everything, no hit needed | Any green | Swarms dying before it ramps |
