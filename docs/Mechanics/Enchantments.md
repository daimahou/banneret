# Energy and enchantments

- Regeneration is shown as pips: one pip represents one energy per beat. Counts differ by hero.
- Maintained enchantments and auras cost energy upfront and reserve a pip while active; dropping one restores it.
- Aura skills also do something immediately when cast.
- Reserving beyond zero is allowed: negative regeneration drains energy each beat.
- At zero energy, all the hero's enchantments end.
- Enchantment is a shared design backbone delivered through class skills; no precursor enchantment skill.
