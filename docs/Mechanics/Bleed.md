## Interactions

| With | Idea |
|---|---|
| [[Ward]] | Bleed ticks ignore Ward |
| [[Armour]] | Armour stops the first cut; Crush and armour break open it |
| [[Stagger]] | Aggravate holds the stacks while the target is Staggered |
| [[Combo]] | Blood trail: Bleed ticks feed combo |
| [[Burn]] | Cauterize: fire closes the wound — combo, or a reason to Rupture first |
| [[Freeze]] | Cold slows the blood: Bleed doesn't tick while Frozen |
| [[Freeze]] | Frozen wounds tear: Shatter on a bleeding target also Ruptures |
| [[Poison]] | Infection: a bleeding, poisoned target can't heal Poison away |
| [[Banners]] | Blood Banner: every party hit applies 1 Bleed — strong with multi-hit skills |
| Blood magic | Feed refills life pips |

## Builds

| Build | Hook | Archetype | Countered by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rupture | Fast weak hits build to a stage, Rupture, reapply | Assassin | Ward, armour, disruption — anything that slows reapplying |
| Feed | Bleed ticks heal him and refill life pips | Reaver | Bloodless enemies, short fights |
| Bleed out | Doom a target, switch, doom the next | Skirmisher | Healers, cleanse |
| Spilled blood | The enemy's blood fuels the party | Warlock, reaver | Bloodless, short fights |
| Bloodlust | The more enemies bleed, the faster he moves | Skirmisher | Cleanse, few enemies |
| Sympathy | Single-target hits become area damage | Hexblade | A lone big enemy |
| Riposte | Enemies bleed themselves on him | Warrior | Ranged and spell attackers |
| Self-bleed | His own wounds power him | Martyr, reaver | Enemy burst while he's low |
