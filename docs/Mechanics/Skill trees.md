# Skill trees

- Base skills are colourless. An identity gate chooses the evolution and deeper tree; the hero must hold its colour.
- Spine: identity → specialisation → capstone. It can be climbed without buying passives.
- Passives branch as a connected tree, unlocked in order.
- Earlier revealed, unpurchased nodes remain available.
- Purchases and identity choices are permanent. No respec.
- Coloured nodes require the matching hero colour; colours are not spent. Nodes cost skill points from camp.
- Minor nodes change damage, delay, cooldown, AP or energy cost. Named notables carry flavour and synergies.
- Nodes may have multiple ranks; investment within a tier may unlock sections.
- Deepen nodes improve a skill's mechanic; cross-colour nodes bring another colour's mechanic into that skill.

> [!frontier] Authored nodes and costs
> Gate colours, node/rank costs, prerequisites and Rupture's placement remain open.

> [!fog] Density and pathing
> Four to six nodes per tier is a layout-test density. About 13 tiers × five nodes ≈ 65 per hero is an estimate. Cross-skill adjacency and a fixed connected tree are outside the current design.

> [!frontier] Skill-pool budget
> Two families per hero felt too restrictive. Author a character's pool before fixing counts.
