# Energy

> [!fog] A pool refilled by regeneration pips, which are a resource too.

> [!fog] An enchantment with an ongoing effect costs a pip of regeneration while it lasts.
> Upkeep instead of a lump sum: a candidate price for Consecration.

> [!fog] Energy conservation: unspent energy carries over, so holding back for a burst turn is a choice.

> [!fog] Regeneration that pays every tick makes the player want to stop whenever a skill becomes affordable.

> [!fog] Comes after [[Action Point (AP)]], once a skill needs a price beyond its cooldown.
