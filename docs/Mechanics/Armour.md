# Armour

- Subtract armour from each hit; fully absorbed hits are possible.
- Damage getting through wears armour by one; a fully absorbed hit leaves it intact.
- Armour break wears two instead of one.
- Inherent to characters, not applied by skills; baggage or campaign effects may add it.
- Hero armour carries between fights. The Smith restores it at camp for gold.

> [!frontier] Armour and repairs
> Enemy values, hero starting armour and Smith prices remain open.
