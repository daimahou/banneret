# Channeling

- A channelled spell evolution becomes an enchantment reserving pips.
- Gains one charge per beat. The caster can act; interrupts do not cancel it.
- Several spells can charge simultaneously.
- Zero energy ends all enchantments and loses their charges.
- Pay spell energy cost on release; it may grow with charges up to a cap.
- Released power scales with charges. Several spells may release together.
- Replaces the earlier caster-lockout version.

> [!frontier] Channel values
> Reserved pips, charge cap, power scaling and release costs remain open. Channelled Fireball is an example.
