# Combo

- One charge resource, capped at five per hero.
- Some skills build it; others spend it for stronger effects.
- Lasts through a fight and resets between fights.
- Mainly rogue, though other heroes may use it.

> [!frontier] Builders and spenders
> Skills, amounts and full-spend payoff remain open.

See [[Chain skills|Chain skills]] for a sequence inside one skill.
