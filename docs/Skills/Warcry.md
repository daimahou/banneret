# Warcry

A Rage builder. Instant: shout, gain [[Rage]] at once, then line up the strike.

- Useful alone: Rage has its own effect, so the cry never needs a Rage spender.
- Any warrior sub-class can hold it; its nodes give it a different identity on each.

## Talents

A red skill. See [[Skill design#Talents]].

| Tier | Talent | Colour | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rally | Balance | Also gives Rage to an ally. A rogue has none of his own, and his fast hits gain more from it |
| 1 | | Strength | |
| 1 | Calm | Light | Adds a second state to the skill: after the cry it flips to Calm, which turns his Rage into a self-heal ([[Catharsis]]). Works like a [[Seals and judgments\|seal and judgment]] |

> [!frontier] The red talent

> [!frontier] Follow-ups and ultimates
> Two follow-ups per talent, deepening it; one ultimate each. Red may offer another red and a purple. Earlier ideas: the cry bleeds him instead of a cooldown; the whole party gets a smaller share; the Rage goes into the blade as charges; more Rage the lower his health.
