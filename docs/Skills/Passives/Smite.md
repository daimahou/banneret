# Smite

## Nodes

> [!frontier] Skill idea
> Yellow, paladin: bonus damage against an enemy whose next action targets anyone except the paladin. Values remain open.

> [!frontier] Ascendancy nodes - Tier 3 ideas
> Fanatic: an ally being hit moves queued Smite earlier. Crusader: trigger a weaker judgment without breaking the seal, or add seal charges to strengthen the eventual judgment. Martyr: more damage for each point of own health lost, possibly paying life instead of energy.
