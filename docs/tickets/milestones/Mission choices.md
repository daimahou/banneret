# Mission choices

Act 1 plays as five missions of two fights each, then Boss 1. Each mission is chosen for its reward and the pressure attached to it. Spec source: [[ideas/Mission choices|Mission choices concept]], [[Baggage|Baggage concept]].

## Done when

- A mission shows on one screen: four steps, in order a difficulty event, a fight, an artifact event, a fight, joined into paths. Every path ends at the same final fight. The player picks a path from what the steps show.
- Every node gives one gem, its colour shown on the map, fights included.
- A fight node shows its encounter type and level, never its enemies.
- The **difficulty event** is the mission's knob: its twist is shown on the map, and visiting the node accepts it. Every path has exactly one, and the player picks which.
- The **artifact event** offers artifacts, revealed on arrival; the player must pick one. Artifacts add run-specific interactions that support several builds. More reward ideas: [[Event reward categories]].
- Every route choice is a trade-off; none is plainly best.
- Routes are chosen for colours: the path to the gems a build needs can run through the fights it handles worst.
- Rewards have no quality tiers: a better tier would always be the right pick. Routes differ through their gems, fights and twists.
- The map shows the current state: fight levels and types include every twist already taken, and each hero's health, energy and cooldowns.
- Twist kinds: **the next fight levels up**, **the next fight brings its type's commander**, **an encounter type is stronger for the rest of the run**, or **Boss 1 is harder**. A type twist is permanent; a mission-long one would weigh no more than the next fight.
- Each encounter type has several twists, each stackable: a buff to its enemies (brigands hit harder; knifemen's hits apply Bleed) or an addition to its fights (a knifeman joins every Brigands fight). A type twist names one; taking it again adds a stack. Twists combine: Bloodthirst grows with every added knifeman.
- Act 1 runs five missions of two fights, then Boss 1. There is no recovery: health, energy and cooldowns all carry from one fight into the next; a party wipe loses the run; defeating Boss 1 clears the act.
- The run's twists are always on screen: each encounter type's total, and the boss's.
- In a fight, enemy intents show final values, every twist included.
