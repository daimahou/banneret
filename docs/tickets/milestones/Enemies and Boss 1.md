# Enemies and Boss 1

Act 1's enemies and its boss. Spec source: [[Prototype/Design concept/Enemies|Enemies concept]], [[Boss complications|Boss complications concept]].

## Done when

- An encounter type is a theme the map shows (e.g. Brigands). It has several instances (e.g. Roadblock, Ambush, Toll gate); Act 1 has at least eight instances in all.
- Enemies are tagged by encounter type and tier: **core** (brigands: brigand, knifeman, …) and **elite**.
- Each type also has a **commander** (brigands: the chief): rare, a big deal, with a mechanic of its own tied to its type, not just combat stats. A commander never comes from the level ladder, only from a difficulty twist. See [[commanders - random rare mobs mini-bosses]].
- An instance's lineup is slots by tier, each filled at random from that tier, so the same instance varies from fight to fight.
- One level ladder for every type, by tier: level 1, a core enemy; level 2, two core; level 3, two core and an elite (the stage is full); level 4, plus a core in the queue; level 5, plus a core then an elite in the queue.
- Instances differ in which enemies fill their tiers (Ambush draws its core mostly from knifemen).
- Enemies are weak one by one; a fight is a stream of them. Up to three are on stage; the rest of the lineup waits in a reinforcement queue and the next one enters when an enemy dies. The next arrival is shown.
- Boss 1 ends the act. It has its own signature mechanic, as Darkest Dungeon's bosses do.
- Boss 1's complications are designed for it alone: each makes its mechanic harder in a specific way. A "Boss 1 is harder" twist adds one, shown on the boss marker.

> [!frontier] Content
> Working count per encounter type: 3 core enemies, 2 elites, 1 commander. To author later: the types, their enemies, at least eight instances, each type's twists.

> [!fog] Boss 1's design
> Its mechanic and complications wait until the tools to choose them exist.
