# Playtest and tune

Play Act 1 end to end and tune it against the gate's [[Act 1 prototype#Playtest|playtest questions]].

## Done when

- Act 1 is played through repeatedly, recording section duration, decision time, health and resources at boss entry, route choices, declined rewards and defeat causes.
- At least two substantially different builds reach the boss.
- Decisions and readability are tuned from what the playtests show.
- The design the playtest keeps is written into the vault.
