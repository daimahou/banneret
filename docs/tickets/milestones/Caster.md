# Caster

The staff precursor and its classes. Spec source: [[Prototype/Design concept/Class paths|Class paths concept]].

## Done when

- The caster starts colourless and becomes a wizard, a priest or a warlock through colour picks, then their ascendancies.
- Each class has enough skills and tree nodes to play as a distinct build.
- The mechanics these classes use work: [[Channeling|channeling]], energy shield, Fire, Renew, [[Curses|curses]], [[Blood magic|blood magic]].
