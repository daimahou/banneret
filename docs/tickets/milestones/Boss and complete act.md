# Boss and complete act

Finish the act with a boss that tests the party built along the route. Play and tune the complete run against [[Act 1 prototype#Playtest|the gate's playtest questions]].

## Done when

- The act's routes culminate in a boss fight, entered with the party's accumulated build, resources, relics and twists.
- Boss complications taken along the route affect the final fight, with their consequences readable before committing and reflected in the boss's actions.
- Defeating the boss clears Act 1; losing the party ends the run in defeat. A player can reach either outcome without developer intervention.
- The content selected in the gate works together throughout the complete act.
- Playtesting demonstrates at least two substantially different builds reaching the boss.
- Combat, offers, routes and events are tuned from playtesting; retained design changes are recorded in the relevant notes.

> [!frontier] Boss design
> What challenge does the boss present, and how do its complications change that challenge?

## Playtest

- Does the final fight test the choices made throughout the act?
- Are failures understandable, and does a successful build deliver a satisfying payoff?
- Do rewards change value with party state and accumulated twists?
- Does accepting later pressure create room to develop now?
