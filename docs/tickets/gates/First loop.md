# First loop

Closed after the initial simulation and Warrior kit work. The remaining event, camp and art milestones were retired in favour of the [[Act 1 prototype|new prototype]].

## Original scope

- A run plays with one hero, the warrior, who has a couple of skills.
- He fights one enemy that has a couple of skills, in two missions with camp between them.
- One event puts up a decision that changes the fight.
- Gold earned in the first mission is spent in camp, and changes the second fight.
- The art is rough but readable, and every skill is staged and tuned for feel.
