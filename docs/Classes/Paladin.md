## Mechanics and ascendancies

- The paladin holds several mechanics; each ascendancy pushes one set of them further, and that set is its identity.
- Each ascendancy is bound to a second colour. It can repeat the precursor's colour.

> [!frontier] The paladin's mechanics
> Which mechanics the class holds for its ascendancies to push. Candidates: seals and judgments, Renew, purify; suffering on behalf of allies; rage when allies are hurt.

> [!fog] More ascendancies
> Three for now; more may come later, from the directions below.

Skill drafts: Smite, Consecration, Communion.

---

# Design Directions

- **Holy**: Healing and blessings to sustain and empower the party.

> [!provisional] Directions
> - Retribution: Punish aggression against the party with holy retaliation.
> - Protector: Shelter allies by building protection against incoming harm.

# Other ideas

- **Scabbard of Light — upgrade:** stores a charge when healed, empowering the next attack. Base attack still undefined.
- **Blade of Mercy — upgrade:** extra damage against poisoned, bleeding or burning enemies. Rewards allies supplying afflictions the Paladin rarely applies.
- **Absolution — upgrade:** killing an afflicted enemy heals the most damaged ally.
    - **Last Confession:** if that ally was below a health threshold, refresh the attack's cooldown. Threshold undecided.
- Guardian angel
- Beacon of light
- Purgatory: fatal damage heal you full and give you a dot for that amount
