# Reaver

**Blood**: he bleeds on purpose. His own [[Bleed]] powers him, the enemy's Bleed heals him, and he moves wounds from one side to the other.

## Mechanic

| Stage | Keywords | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| Open | [[Self-bleed]], [[Riposte]], [[Internal bleeding]] | He cuts himself and bleeds whoever hits him; heavy blows open armoured targets |
| Profit | [[Feed]], [[Leech]], [[Last stand]] | Enemy ticks and his own hits heal him; low health makes him hit harder |
| Trade | [[Blood for blood]], [[Spilled blood]] | His stacks, at their stage, go onto an enemy; ticks fill a pool the whole party can spend |
| Pay | [[Blood magic]] | His enchantments reserve life pips: Feed is what keeps them up |

| Strong against | Weak against |
|---|---|
| Fleshy swarms: more bleeders, more healing | Bloodless and heavily armoured enemies |
| Attrition fights where his sustain outlasts the enemy | Anti-heal: [[Rot]], [[Starve]] |
| Enemies that hit him often ([[Riposte]]) | Burst while he sits at low health on purpose |
| | His own healer: [[Bind]] closes the wounds he wants open |

## Skill seeds

| Skill | Idea |
|---|---|
| Reaving Strike | Dark evolution of [[Strike]]: heals for part of the damage dealt ([[Leech]]). Notables: Blood Strike (pays health for damage), Despair Strike (more damage the lower the target). |
| Bloodletting | He cuts himself: several stacks of [[Self-bleed]], no enemy involved, so nothing can block it. While he bleeds his hits [[Leech]] more, and more again at [[Aggravated]]. |
| Blood for Blood | A hit that moves all his Bleed onto the target, keeping its stage. Bleed himself to [[Aggravated]], hand it over, then [[Rupture]]. Works on an ally's Bleed too. |

> [!frontier] Does his own Bleed hurt him at full strength?
> If Feed and Leech outheal it too easily, Self-bleed is free; if not, he needs a healer who then [[Bind]]s it away.
