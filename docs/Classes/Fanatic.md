# Fanatic

**Fanatism**: dangerous devotion, forbidden blessings and shared rituals. Commit the party to an offensive whose power comes with obligations and health risks. #shadow

# Ideas

- **Fanaticism nodes:** relentless, faster attacks as the payoff. Trigger ideas vary by role: allies hit, own health lost, hitting cursed or debuffed enemies, or holding a seal.
- **False Miracle**: Delay an ally's death from lethal damage for a few beats. Killing the condemned enemy saves them; otherwise they die when the delay ends. Duration remains open.
