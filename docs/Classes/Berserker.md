# Berserker

**Rage**: [[Oath of fury]] replaces his mana with [[Rage]]. He is strongest near full Rage and punished for going over; the fight is about staying in that band.

## Mechanic

| Stage | Keywords | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| Build | [[Bloodied]], [[Grudge]], [[Rampage]], [[Warcry]] | Rage comes from being hit, hitting back and killing: he wants to be in the middle |
| Ride | [[Fury]], [[Frenzy]], [[Unyielding]], [[Reckless]] | High Rage hits harder, faster and can't be staggered, at the cost of armour |
| Tip over | [[Boiling over]], [[Berserk]], [[Uncontrolled]] | Full Rage is the danger zone: spend before it spills |
| Outlast | [[Painless]], [[Undying]] | Rage delays damage and death; when it runs out, the bill lands |

| Strong against | Weak against |
|---|---|
| Swarms and reinforcement queues: every kill refuels him | Stalling: [[Stagger]], [[Freeze]], [[Block]]-heavy enemies, gaps between arrivals, so Rage drains unused |
| Long brawls with constant exchanges | Burst while [[Reckless]] has stripped his armour |
| Stagger-based enemies, once [[Unyielding]] | [[Calm]], and anything that makes him overshoot full |

## Skill seeds

| Skill | Idea |
|---|---|
| [[Warcry]] | Gain Rage at once, plus extra for each enemy whose next action targets him. Nodes: [[Fervour]] (his Rage spills to the party), [[Mortal enemy]] (the cry names a target), a version that taunts. |
| Onslaught | Spender. Drains all his Rage: one hit on the same target per chunk of Rage spent, each hit [[Relentless]]. A kill refunds through [[Rampage]] and the leftover hits [[Carry through]]. The safe way down from full. |
| Bloodthirst | No cooldown; costs Rage instead, and each use in a row costs more. Lets him attack every beat while the Rage lasts. |

> [!frontier] Does going past full punish the hero, or the player?
> [[Boiling over]] hurts him; [[Uncontrolled]] and [[Berserk]] take choices away. One should be the class default, the others nodes.
