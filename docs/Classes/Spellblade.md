# Spellblade

**Charged blade**: his weapon is the spell. He [[Channeling|channels]] into the sword while he keeps fighting, and chooses which hit releases it as [[Magic]] damage.

![[Skills.base#Skills]]

![[Classes.base#Sub-classes]]

## Mechanic

| Stage | Keywords | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| Charge | [[Channeling]], [[Overcharge]], [[Attunement]] | Reserved pips build charges each beat while he swings normally |
| Release | [[Magic]], [[Exposed]] | The chosen hit carries every charge and ignores armour |
| Brand | [[Frost brand]], [[Kindle]] | The element the blade holds decides what plain hits leave behind |
| Break | [[Frozen]], [[Shatter]], [[Crush]] | A warrior's heavy blow is the Crush that shatters what his own blade froze |

| Strong against | Weak against |
|---|---|
| Armoured enemies: Magic goes straight through | Energy denial: [[Leak]], [[Silence]], [[Sap]], then [[Burnout]] drops the charge |
| Slow heavy enemies that give him time to charge | [[Ward]] and energy-shield enemies soaking the release |
| | Swarms: a big release on a small enemy is wasted [[Overkill]] |

## Skill seeds

| Skill | Idea |
|---|---|
| [[Arcane Strike]] | Blue evolution of [[Strike]]: Magic damage, ignores armour. Carries the release. |
| Imbue | The channel. Reserves a pip, one charge per beat; his next weapon skill spends them all as bonus Magic damage. Nodes pick a brand: frost (plain hits [[Chill]]) or fire (plain hits [[Kindle]]). |
| Shattering Blow | Slow [[Crush]] hit. On a [[Frozen]] target it [[Shatter]]s; released with charges, the burst [[Splash]]es. |

> [!frontier] Does releasing empty the blade, or can he hold charges through a hit?
> Holding lets him fight with plain hits while a big release waits for the right target; always-release is simpler to read.
