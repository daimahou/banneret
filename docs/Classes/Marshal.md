# Marshal

**Formations**: a [[Banners|banner]] with two [[Forms|forms]], guard and press, swapped for free. He reads [[Intent]] on the timeline and puts the party in the right one before the blow lands.

## Mechanic

| Form | Keywords | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| Guard | [[Interpose]], [[Shieldwall]], [[Brace]], [[Shield an ally]] | Hits aimed at allies land on him or on his armour instead |
| Press | [[Reprisal]], [[Intercept]], [[Avenge]] | The party punishes whoever is aiming at an ally, sooner and harder |
| Either | [[Backlash]], [[Shield slam]] | What he absorbed comes back as damage |

| Strong against | Weak against |
|---|---|
| Telegraphed heavy hitters and bosses | Damage over time: ticks ignore every defence |
| Enemies that focus one fragile ally | [[Magic]] and [[Area]] attacks: nothing to interpose |
| | [[Rust]], [[Armour break]], and energy drain on the pips his banner reserves |
| | Many small fast hits: swapping can't keep up |

## Skill seeds

| Skill | Idea |
|---|---|
| Hold the Line | The formation banner. On cast: [[Block]] to every hero covered. Guard form: his armour also protects covered allies ([[Shieldwall]]). Press form: party hits on an enemy aiming at an ally deal more ([[Reprisal]]). |
| Cover | The next attack aimed at an ally is redirected to him ([[Interpose]]) and he gains Block against it. Node: a fully blocked hit damages the attacker ([[Backlash]]). |
| Counterblow | A hit that deals damage equal to the Block and armour he holds, with [[Stagger]]. Best queued right after the enemy's hit, before Block fades. |

> [!frontier] Is swapping form really free, or once per beat?
> Free swapping lets him hold both forms' benefits within one beat on a continuous timeline.
