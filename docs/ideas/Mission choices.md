# Mission choices

1. Inspect available mission choices.
2. Compare reward, immediate threat and lasting baggage.
3. Commit to a mission.
4. Resolve two fights, camping after each.
5. Develop the party and continue toward the boss.

- Central choice: is this reward worth the pressure attached to it?
- Routes offer different problems and development opportunities.
- The hardest optional route must not become the mandatory source of superior power.
- Some choices add ongoing pressure; others defer complications to a boss.

See [[Baggage|Baggage]], [[Boss complications|Boss complications]] and [[02 - Presentation|02 - Presentation]].
