# Boss complications

- Bosses keep recognizable core mechanics across runs.
- Selected baggage adds a clearly described complication at a specified checkpoint.
- Respect the small on-stage enemy count through free slots or a reinforcement queue.
- Deferred complications change the timing of pressure; they do not automatically favour burst builds.

> [!frontier] Prototype complication
> Proposed limit: one per checkpoint. Alternatives: two waves before the boss arrives, or reinforcements at half health. Reinforcements trigger once even if health crosses the threshold again.

> [!frontier] Reinforcement inheritance
> State whether summoned regular enemies inherit ordinary baggage.
