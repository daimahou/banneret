Hired followers, dropped from the prototype's party wheel.

- A run would hold three followers, part of the build.
- A map reward could leave the party with none or too many, so they would come at fixed moments instead.

# Followers

- A follower joins through their [[Arcs|arc]]: the arc's first step rallies them.
- Each step of the arc lets you pick one bonus from the follower's next tier. Level-ups never grow a follower.
- Followers don't fight.

## Smith — [[Arcs|The forge]]

| Tier | Picks |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Gear 1** — weapon and armour cap 1 |
| 2 | **Gear 2** — weapon and armour cap 2 |
| 3 | **Weapons 3** — weapon cap 3 · **Armour 3** — armour cap 3 |

- Weapon: +1 damage per tier on each of the hero's hits. Armour: +5 most health per tier.
- At a camp, gold buys a hero's next tier, up to the Smith's cap.

## Healer — The jail

| Tier | Picks |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Field dressing** — heroes heal 4 after each fight |
| 2 | **Bleed ward** — Bleed on a hero loses 2 stacks a beat · **Poison ward** — Poison on a hero deals 1 less |
| 3 | **Surgeon's hands** — Field dressing heals 8 · **Triage** — a hero under half health after a fight heals to half |

## Alchemist — The well

| Tier | Picks |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Healing draught** — each hero gains the skill Draught: heal 8, once per fight |
| 2 | **Antidote** — Draught also clears Poison · **Iron skin** — Draught also gives Ward 6 |
| 3 | **Cheap brews** — Draught twice per fight · **Strong brews** — Draught heals 14 |
