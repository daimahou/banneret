# Camp

- Camp follows every fight; the party levels up and spends camp time.
- A small shared pool of points can be split between heroes or concentrated on one.
- One point buys one activity for a hero or follower: train for a skill point, heal, repair armour at the Smith for gold, or another follower service.
- Recovery competes with training. Followers use the same pool.
- Hired followers determine available services. Camp actions grant no extra points.
- Target: fairly even development, roughly a third to half of each hero's trees; tier gates limit concentration without a hard allocation cap.

> [!frontier] Camp budget
> The later working value is two shared points per camp. The earlier estimate of roughly 96 skill points does not match 21 camps × two = 42 activity points before recovery. Reconcile before using either as a target.

> [!frontier] Service list and follower progression
> Service strength, repair prices, follower camp actions and progression remain open.
