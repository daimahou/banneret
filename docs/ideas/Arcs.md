# Arcs

- An arc has 3 steps. The first is a map node you choose to visit. Each later step appears on the next mission's map.
- Each step is a fight against the lord's men. The third step, the **climax**, is a fight against the lord in person.
- After each step's fight, pick the follower's next bonus. The first step rallies them. See [[Followers 1|Followers 1]].
- **The lord's response:** each step adds one stack of the lord's baggage. Winning the climax removes all of it.
- If you skip an arc's next step, the arc fades: no more steps, and its baggage stays.
- Missions 1 and 2 offer new arcs to start, two per map. The boss mission has no arc steps.

| Arc | Follower | Lord | Lord's baggage, per stack |
|---|---|---|---|
| The forge | Smith | The Marshal | Regular enemies start each fight with Ward 3 |
| The jail | Healer | The Warden | Regular enemies' Stagger is 1 higher |
| The well | Alchemist | The Steward | Poison applied by regular enemies is 1 higher |
