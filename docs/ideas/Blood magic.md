# Blood magic

- Enchantments reserve life pips instead of energy pips.
- Health drains each beat instead of reducing energy regeneration.
- Heroes have no natural health regeneration; healing and regeneration effects can offset the drain.
- Delivered through purple nodes in individual skills.

> [!frontier] Authored costs
> Life-paid seals and Agony are examples. Drain values and individual effects remain open.
