# Baggage

- Rules accepted through route choices; distinct from temporary hero debuffs.
- Strengthens existing enemy effects or adds side effects to named enemy types.
- Accumulates through the run unless a rule states a shorter lifetime.
- Changes abilities and effectiveness; increasing enemy count is not the default.
- Each rule states target scope, affected ability/type, value, stacking behaviour and lifetime.
- Ordinary baggage does not automatically modify bosses.
- Apply once after commitment; cancelled or failed selection applies nothing.

> [!frontier] Poison stacking example
> Stronger venom adds two poison to regular-enemy poison skills; poisoned arrows adds one to archer basic attacks. Combined result of three is proposed. Define eligibility, order and caps centrally.

> [!frontier] Initial themes
> Poison and armour are proposed. Armour baggage can grant armour to an enemy type or raise its value; types and values remain open.

See [[Boss complications|Boss complications]].

- **Baggage** is a rule the band takes on by choosing a map node. The node shows it before you travel there.
- A node with baggage gives 2 gems instead of 1.
- Baggage stays for the rest of the run, unless its rule says otherwise.
- It changes **regular enemies** only, never a lord or the boss.
- Taking the same baggage twice adds up: two Stronger venom make enemy Poison 4 higher.
- Enemy skills show their final numbers, baggage included.
- Active baggage is listed at the top of the screen.

| Baggage | Rule |
|---|---|
| Stronger venom | Poison applied by regular enemies is 2 higher |
| Poisoned arrows | Archers' hits also apply Poison 1 |
| Mail | Regular enemies start each fight with Ward 4 |
| Whetted blades | Regular enemies' hits deal 1 more damage |
| Drilled | Regular enemies' skills resolve 1 tick sooner, but never sooner than 1 tick |
| Bloodthirst | Brigands' hits also apply Bleed 1 |

Each arc's lord adds baggage of their own. See [[Arcs|Arcs]].

## Boss complications

- A few nodes offer a **boss complication** instead of baggage: it changes the boss fight only. The node gives 2 gems, as baggage does.
- The band can hold only **one** complication. Once it has one, no other is offered.
- The complication shows on the boss's map node.

| Complication | Rule |
|---|---|
| Outriders | Two Riders fight the band before the Sheriff arrives: the boss fight starts with a wave of Riders |
| Reinforcements | When the Sheriff first drops to half health, two Riders join the fight. It happens once. |

- Enemies a complication brings are regular enemies, so baggage applies to them.
