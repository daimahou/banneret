# Combat and recovery

- Three heroes; timeline resolution with activation delays, cooldowns, AP and energy costs.
- Roughly three enemies on stage as the working encounter size.
- Party wipe loses the run.
- Waves are optional, serving specific encounter purposes.
- Health and relevant limited resources carry between fights.

> [!frontier] Cooldown and energy recovery
> Proposed test: retain cooldowns and recover a configurable amount at a travel boundary; compare with full resets. Prevent unlimited recovery by waiting outside fights. Amount, timing unit and boundaries remain open. The later camp section assigns recovery activities to camp without specifying these rules.

> [!fog] Burst versus sustain
> Test whether recovery makes expensive, long-cooldown skills meaningfully different from repeatable attacks and sustain.
