# Artifacts

- Support several builds rather than dictate one named skill.
- Early artifacts can influence identity; later ones can support unchosen upgrades, another hero or an existing interaction.
- No respec or artifact reroll system in the first version.

> [!frontier] Initial offering and scope
> Proposed: four distinct effects, an early choice of three, and one later Act 1 offer. Passive party-wide effects lasting until section end are a proposed default. Capacity and replacement remain open. Avoid gear inventory, random affixes and equipment slots in this prototype proposal.

> [!frontier] Decline reward
> The alternative and amount remain open; earlier sphere rewards predate removal of drops.

> [!frontier] Coins and equipment progression
> Simple equipment levels bought with coins were discussed; scope, costs and future currency uses remain open.

- An artifact node offers **3 artifacts** you don't have yet. Take one, or decline them all for **2 gems** of the node's kind.
- An artifact works for the whole party until the section ends.
- There is no limit to how many you hold.

| Artifact | Effect |
|---|---|
| Whetstone | The heroes' hits deal 1 more damage |
| Serpent's fang | Poison the heroes apply is 1 higher |
| Tinderbox | Fire ignites at 4 flammability instead of 5 |
| Bloodstone | Bleed the heroes apply is 1 higher |
| Martyr's relic | The heroes' healing heals 2 more |
| War drum | Each hero starts every fight with Ward 4 |

> [!fog] An artifact that rewards repeated hits, shared by several heroes and skills.
